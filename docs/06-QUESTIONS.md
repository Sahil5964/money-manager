# Questions the Product Should Answer

The product is ultimately valuable because of the questions it can answer. Feature work should be evaluated against this list.

## Money movement

- How much money left my accounts?
- Where did my money go?
- How much did I spend this month?
- How much did I receive?
- How much moved between my own accounts?

## Category and merchant

- How much did I spend on groceries?
- How much did I spend at Blinkit?
- Which merchants increased my spending?
- Which recurring charges do I have?

## People

- How much did I spend for my mother/father/brother?
- How much have I gifted my family?
- How much has my family benefited from my spending?
- How much does each person currently owe me?
- Who do I currently owe?

## Shared money

- How much of this purchase was mine?
- Which spending was household/shared?
- Which expenses are expected to be reimbursed?
- What reimbursements are overdue or unresolved?

## Products

- How much paneer did I buy?
- Which brand of paneer do I buy most?
- How much do I spend on milk per month?
- Which products have increased most in price?
- Which products make up most of my grocery basket?

## Consumption

- What am I actually consuming?
- How much paneer do I consume per month?
- Has my consumption changed over time?
- What is my typical weekly grocery basket?
- What fraction of grocery spending is household vs personal consumption?

## Context and intent

- How much did the trip cost?
- How much did I spend on birthdays/celebrations?
- How much of my spending was for work?
- How much was family support vs personal spending?

## Lifecycle and obligations

- How much is still owed to me?
- How much have I prepaid?
- How much of last month's spending was later refunded?
- What recurring payments are coming next?
- Which commitments have changed in price?

## Behavior and trends

- Why did spending increase this month?
- What changed compared with my normal pattern?
- Which spending categories are growing fastest?
- What am I buying more frequently?
- What do I repeatedly buy without realizing the total cost?

## Forecasting

- What will my likely cash flow be next month?
- What recurring expenses are coming?
- When will I reach a savings goal?
- What happens if my income changes?
- What happens if I increase a recurring investment?

## What-if analysis

- Can I afford this purchase without breaking my goals?
- What if I reduce dining out by 20%?
- What if I stop a subscription?
- What if I take a six-month break from income?

## Product design rule

When proposing a new data point, explicitly name at least one useful question it enables.

When proposing a complex workflow, explain why the same questions cannot be answered adequately without that complexity.
